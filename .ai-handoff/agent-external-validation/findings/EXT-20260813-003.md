intent (e.g., `ok ok, I want to go back and continue — let's benchmark
ethereum`) are rejected with the generic "clarify unmapped" template,
even when the pending question is `Which chain do you want to
benchmark?` and the sentence clearly contains a chain name that is
supported. The parser does not extract the chain from a chatty
sentence; it only accepts the bare chain token (`ethereum`).
